## Gemini: endpoints and what the live API really returns

Used (public, no key):

- `GET https://api.gemini.com/v1/prediction-markets/events?status=active&category=sports&search=<text>&limit=50`
- `GET https://api.gemini.com/v1/prediction-markets/events/{eventTicker}` (404 with `{"error":"Event not found"}` for an unknown ticker)

Differences from the PRD, found with curl before coding:

1. Lists are wrapped: `{ "data": [events], "pagination": { limit, offset, total } }`. A single event comes bare.
2. `search` matches **titles and tickers**, not team nicknames. Titles use the city: `Tennessee vs Dallas`, `Los Angeles C vs Baltimore`. Searching `49ers` or `Cowboys` returns mostly college teams, so the client translates NFL teams to Gemini's city names and searches `"<away city> vs <home city>"`, which returns all nine sibling markets in one call (about 265 KB, 0.5 s).
3. A contract's `ticker` is short (`DAL`, `D` for a tie, `O53` for over 53.5); `instrumentSymbol` is the long one (`GEMI-NFL-2611221800-TEN-DAL-M-DAL`).
4. Events carry `participants[]` (name, abbreviation, home/away) and `sportsMarket { type, scope { type, ordinal } }`. Classification uses those and falls back to the ticker suffix (`-M`, `-1QM`, `-1HM`, `-T`, `-S`).
5. Ticker order is away then home, and the 10-digit block is `YYMMDDHHMM` in UTC.
6. Extra market types exist that we ignore: team totals (`-TT`), period totals and spreads, player props (`-PPTD` and others), season win totals.
7. Quarter and half markets are listed days ahead but had **no bids or asks** for the Nov 22 game on Nov 16, so they show as unavailable until books fill in closer to kickoff. The Nov 17 game (LAC at BAL) already had them.
8. The web link is `https://www.gemini.com/predictions/{eventTicker}/{slug}` (checked: the page title is "Tennessee vs Dallas | Gemini Predictions").
9. The PRD example `NFL-2611152125-SF-DAL-M` is real but **settled on Nov 15** and has no prices. See decision A3-1.

## What is mocked, untrained or unverified

- The penalty career tallies are approximate and hand-entered (flagged in the JSON). They move the hero market from 74% to 83%.
- The measured penalty base rate (74%) is slightly under the 75 to 80% the brief expected. I kept the measured number and set the sanity test to 70 to 80%.
- ESPN soccer penalty parsing ("Penalty X. Y draws a foul...") is tested on **synthetic** commentary lines; the recorded match had no penalty.
- ESPN and API-Football adapters have not been watched through a real live game. Polling, de-duplication and history handling are tested with fake fetches only.
- Corner outcomes from ESPN are inferred (a shot on target by the same team within 30 s of game clock).
- The offline Gemini mock uses invented sample prices, clearly flagged.
- No LLM was called. The ranker was exercised with a fake client only, so the prompt's real-world output quality is untested.

## Decisions

| # | Decision | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A3-1 | The NFL replay is Tennessee at Dallas, not San Francisco vs Dallas as the brief suggested. | SF at DAL was played Nov 15 and is settled on Gemini. TEN at DAL (Nov 22) is listed with live prices for the whole hackathon week, so the Gemini panel matches for real. |
| A3-2 | Gemini matching needs the league and **both** teams; one team alone is never enough. | Showing another game's odds next to our market would be misleading. |
| A3-3 | Unreachable Gemini falls back to flagged sample odds by default; no match hides the column. `GEMINI_FALLBACK=none` hides it in both cases. | PRD: the demo must run offline, and a missing market must be said out loud. |
| A3-4 | Penalty market locks 20 s after it opens (PRD says kick taken / 45 s). | In demo time the kick comes 27 s after the award; 20 s gives a locked beat before the result. |
| A3-5 | "Another card before halftime" locks 30 s after opening (PRD says halftime); next goal locks on the goal or at the 85th minute. | Otherwise a late "No" (or "none") bet is nearly free money in pool mode, where the price does not move. |
| A3-6 | `lock.onEvent` accepts alternatives separated by a vertical bar, for example `goal` or `minute:85` in one string. | One field had to express "whichever comes first". |
| A3-7 | Corners use two events: the award (no `shotOnTarget`) and the outcome (with it). | A market cannot resolve from the event that created it. |
| A3-8 | A `snap` event is the pre-snap situation and opens the market; it locks after 15 s, and the `play_result` resolves it. | Live feeds only report a play after it ends, so the end of the previous play is the only usable trigger. |
| A3-9 | 4th-down price = P(go for it) x P(convert), unless the feed says `goForIt`; no market when that is under 10% or the feed says punt/kick. | Most 4th downs are punts; a 4% market is not fun. |
| A3-10 | The rationale is stored in `resolver.params.rationale`. | `Market` has no rationale field and `explain(market)` needs it after a restart. |
| A3-11 | No proposals for events flagged `data.catchUp` (replay seek, live history). | The moment has already passed. |
| A3-12 | Seek backward replays the prefix from seq 1 instead of only moving a cursor. | Counters cannot be rebuilt otherwise; `reduce` resets itself when `seq` goes backwards. |
| A3-13 | Soccer keeps the PRD default goal rates (1.35 / 1.10) rather than the sample means. | The PRD names them; the sample is tournament-heavy. |
| A3-14 | The ranker checks numbers, length and loss-chasing phrases in code, not only in the prompt. | "The LLM never sets a price" should not depend on the LLM obeying. |

## Notes for the engine (A2)

- Call order assumed per event: `reduce`, then countdown locks, `shouldLock`, `evaluate`, then `propose`. Set `market.createdAtSeq = event.seq`; resolvers ignore that event and anything before it.
- Pass every draft, open and locked market in `open`; a template with one of those is not proposed again.
- Honour `candidate.lockAfterSecs` (15 for conversions and run/pass, 20 for penalty, corner, drive and red zone, 30 for card and period winners). The replays leave 21 s or more between moment triggers, so the one-per-20-s limit drops nothing.
- Optional: call `engine.calibrate(game, panel.events)` after `gemini.matchGame(game)` and before kickoff. The NFL total line then follows Gemini (53.5 this week) and `refFor` finds the same line.
- `refFor` returns a ref only when the Gemini contract is the same bet (same period, same total line) and has a usable price.
